    FAQ

    Questions we get every week

    How much does pool service cost around Boca Raton?

    Most residential pools here land between $115 and $185 a month for weekly service, depending on size and whether it's salt or chlorine. The estimator above will give you a number for your pool in about twenty seconds.

    How often does a pool really need servicing in Florida?

    Weekly. Heat, rain and pollen move water chemistry faster here than almost anywhere else in the country, and two weeks between visits is usually where algae gets a foothold. Bi-weekly can work in cooler months or on a screened pool.

    My pool is green. Do I have to drain it?

    Almost never, and you generally shouldn't. Florida's water table is high enough that an empty shell can lift out of the ground. Nearly every green pool comes back with chemical treatment and filtration over a few days.

    What happens if something breaks?
