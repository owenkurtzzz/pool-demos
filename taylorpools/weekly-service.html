At Taylor Pools we pay special attention to the chemistry of your pool to maintain water quality, protect your health, and the maximize the life of your pool's interior surfaces and mechanical equipment. Without proper balancing, water can become corrosive or scaling and the disinfection process becomes inefficient - often prompting over-chlorination. The results can be uncomfortable for swimmers and costly for the homeowner. Cloudy water, discoloration and staining of your pool surface, scaling on tiles, shortened pump and filter life, unpleasant odor and red eyes are just a few of the consequences.

An ounce of prevention is truly worth a pound of cure when it comes to your pool water chemistry. Contact us today to see how clear your water can be.
